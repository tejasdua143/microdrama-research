Show-world fit: The Duke's Masked Bride and The Royal Accident

25 November 2026. Knowledge layer: US micro drama world trope matrix and US micro drama genre demand 2026. Scripts: the original promo scripts for both shows.

Bottom line. Both shows are already built on the engines the market rewards, and both sit in skins the data marks as weak. The Duke's Masked Bride runs Contract marriage, Protector / bodyguard and a hidden-heiress reveal inside a Regency-style duke's court, a world whose 2026 releases have a 0.55M median. The Royal Accident runs Secret baby, Hidden identity and a custody contract inside modern royalty, a pillar with 2 titles and a 16M median. Move The Duke's Masked Bride to mafia / Italian-American first (8.2 of 10), then billionaire / hidden heiress (7.4) and dragon / ancient-kingdom (7.2). Move The Royal Accident to billionaire / secret-baby CEO first (8.4), then dragon / ancient-kingdom (8.0) and mafia / Italian-American (7.6). Of the worlds already tried, the data backs both dragon versions on momentum, half-backs the werewolf and vampire versions, and does not back the witch or Greek myth versions.

How the scores work

Each candidate pillar gets three scores from 1 to 10, then a weighted total out of 10.

FactorWeightWhat it measuresSource
Trope match40%How many of the show's top tropes have filled cells in that pillar's row, how many titles sit in each cell, and the best title's views. A cell with 3+ titles and a best title above 100M scores high; a single-title cell counts as an anecdote.Matrix A and B, proven combos, gaps
Market momentum35%In-window (May 1 to Nov 25, 2026) views summed for the pillar, its rank in the top 15, the direction label and the report's confidence label.Part 1 pillar tables, top-15 ranking
Adaptation ease25%How many of the show's core devices carry over without a rewrite, and which ones break under the new world's rules.Script read, reskin pairs, past audit notes

Trope match carries the most weight because the report's conclusion is that "the world matters less than the reveal schedule." Momentum comes second because both shows need a skin that is growing, not one with a deep back catalog to compete against. Every view figure is a global, platform-reported counter. ReelShort titles show the HaveDrama figure and the app or Vertical Charts figure as a range.

Both scripts are opening promo scripts, not full series: The Duke's Masked Bride is about 6,200 words with five promo starts (PS_01 to PS_05) and ends on "Find out what happens next"; The Royal Accident is about 5,500 words and ends on an install call to action. The trope stacks below describe the opening, and a long series may add tropes these scripts do not show.

1. The Duke's Masked Bride (TDMB, IU252 Contract Marriage V2)

What the script contains

ElementReadEvidence from the script
LoglineA baron's scarred, masked daughter, abused by her stepfamily and offered as payment for her father's debts, proposes a loveless contract marriage to a duke who came to her father's ball to investigate missing women, not to choose a bride."What about a contract?" / "I agree to your terms, but I have a few of my own."
Core trope stack 1. Contract marriage (the engine; she proposes it, he adds terms).
2. Public humiliation (stepmother, stepsister and the other maidens mock her; father sides with the stepsister).
3. Protector / bodyguard (the Duke against William Lancaster, who collects women).
4. Hidden identity (two layers: the mask hides her face behind a false curse, and she is the legal owner of the estate her family lives on).
Secondary: Revenge (set up, not started), and a substitute-bride shape: the Duke came for Kate and ends up with her outcast sister.		"Get away from me, you hungry little witch!" / "Can you imagine? A man like Duke Collins with the cursed daughter of a baron!" / "her mother left all her fortune, and the Barret Estate, in this little runt's name." / "She could make a fine payment for your debts." / "Kate must impress the Duke"
Opening hookA hungry ten-year-old begs her stepmother for food, gets slapped, and eats bread thrown on the floor. Her stepsister shoves her into mud and scars her face with spiked jewelry while her father rushes to help the stepsister."Here," Katherine said, tossing a chunk of bread onto the floor.
Central revealThe Duke does not want a wife; he is there for the missing women. The cook she trusts is reporting to her stepmother. She sees the Duke is "trapped just like me" and offers him a contract."I'm here for information on those missing women" / "The dumb girl has no idea what you and the Baron are planning."
Status gapOutcast "cursed" daughter of an indebted baron vs a duke who owns nearly as much land as the king."That man has as much power as the King"
Setting / eraUnnamed kingdom, a king, a duke, a baron's castle, carriages on cobblestone, "southern elegance" on the estate. Regency-style historical court, grounded, no magic. The curse is a lie the stepmother spreads."Her mother was a witch who cursed her face" / "anyone who looks at her dies."
Lead POVFemale (Alessandra), third person, with cutaways to the Duke.

The report flags two risks inside this stack. "Family is the villain" shows average 64.4 on the Vertical 52 against 72.6 for child-centered shows, and long humiliation loops are a named fatigue pattern. TDMB's first act is almost all family abuse, so any reskin should keep the abuse for the ad and the first episode, then get to the contract and the first reversal fast.

2. The Royal Accident (TRA, promo "Mr. Cortez")

What the script contains

ElementReadEvidence from the script
LoglineDrugged by her half-sister on her birthday, Amelia sleeps with a stranger, signs an NDA, is disowned when she refuses to abort, and signs a support contract that lets the father claim custody and move her in. Six years later the father, the crown prince of Spain, takes their son, and says he never knew the boy existed."I got pregnant by a billionaire. My family kicked me out. I am bound by his contract."
Core trope stack 1. Secret baby (the engine).
2. Hidden identity (the stranger is the crown prince; his paternity must stay secret).
3. Contract marriage, in its contract-cohabitation form: a custody contract that requires her to live in his home, with a bed clause. No wedding yet.
4. Revenge (what the cold open sells).
Secondary: Affair / cheating (the half-sister who drugged her now dates her ex), Second chance (reunion with Javier, and with Charles), Protector / bodyguard (his security, his enemies).		"my child is my weapon for revenge" / "you, along with the child, must reside in my client's home." / "the contract requires you to share my bed" / "I am Javier Calderon, the crown prince of Spain." / "Wait. Is Charles dating my sister?"
Opening hookA first-person revenge promise, then a naked morning-after: armed guards sweep her room, a lawyer makes her sign an NDA, and the man is already on a jet."Please sign this NDA stating you won't attempt to defame or blackmail my client."
Central revealThe father is royalty, and both of them are victims of a conspiracy: someone else signed the contract and paid the support."We are both victims of a conspiracy." / "I never agreed to that contract."
Status gapA disowned single mom and intern translator who put back her own shampoo to afford her son's, vs a crown prince with a private-island estate."Max's father lives here." She had guessed the man was wealthy, but she hadn't expected private island rich.
Setting / eraContemporary Miami: nightclub, apartment with a broken elevator, foreign-affairs council internship, gala, private island villa. Grounded."I'm leading a defense project and serving as an ambassador."
Lead POVFemale (Amelia): first-person cold open, then third person, with cutaways to Javier.

The earlier creative audit of this script found that the cold open sells revenge, minute 21 turns Javier into a co-victim, and the final cliff is love-triangle jealousy with no link back to the drugging. The data adds weight to that note: Affair / cheating is graded "hook, not engine," and the report says regret and betrayal work as an opener, not as the arc. The custody contract and the conspiracy are the scheduled reveals that should carry episodes 10 to 60.
